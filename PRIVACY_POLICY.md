# Privacy Policy

**App Name:** Arrow Escape

**Effective Date:** April 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** April 20, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Arrow Escape is a sliding block puzzle game. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

Arrow Escape is designed with privacy in mind. We do **not** collect:

- Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Camera or microphone data
- Contacts or address book data
- Financial or payment information
- Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID)
- Usage analytics or crash reports sent to our servers
- Any data transmitted to external servers

### 2.2 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Arrow Escape stores the following data **only on your device** using local storage (SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device:

| Data                           | Purpose                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Level completion status        | Track which levels you have completed |
| Best move count per level      | Display your personal best score      |
| Star ratings per level         | Show your achievement on each level   |
| Best completion time per level | Track your fastest solve time         |

This data is stored locally and is only accessible by the app on your device.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy

Arrow Escape is suitable for all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or from anyone. Since no personal data is collected, this app complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations.

---

## 4. Data Security

Since Arrow Escape does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being exposed through our app. All gameplay progress is stored exclusively on your device and is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data (level progress, scores, times) is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by:

- Clearing the app's data in your device's **Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape → Clear Data**
- Uninstalling the app

We have no access to this data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

---

## 6. Permissions

Arrow Escape does not request any special device permissions. The app does not require access to:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Location
- Contacts
- Storage (beyond its own private app directory)
- Phone state
- Network

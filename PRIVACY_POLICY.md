## 1. Overview

Block Escape X: Unblock Puzzle (the "App") is an offline sliding block puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, why, and the choices you have.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

---

## 2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The App stores the following gameplay data **only on your device** using local storage (SharedPreferences). We cannot access it, and it is never sent to us:

| Data                           | Purpose                               |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Level completion status        | Track which levels you have completed |
| Best move count per level      | Display your personal best score      |
| Star ratings per level         | Show your achievement on each level   |
| Best completion time per level | Track your fastest solve time         |

## 3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads. Ads are served by **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK), a third-party advertising service provided by Google LLC. When ads are requested, AdMob may automatically collect and process:

- **Device identifiers**, such as your Android Advertising ID
- **Device and app information**, such as device model, operating system version, app version, and language
- **Approximate location** derived from your IP address
- **Ad interaction data**, such as ad impressions and clicks
- **Diagnostic data**, such as ad loading performance and crash information related to the ads SDK

This data is used to deliver ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and (where you allow it) personalize ads. It is processed by Google under its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive personally identifying information from AdMob.

### Your ad choices

- **Reset or delete your Advertising ID:** Android **Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, depending on your device).
- **Opt out of personalized ads:** use the same settings screen. You will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.
- **EEA, UK, and Switzerland:** where required, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using a Google-certified consent message. You can change your choice from the App's privacy options where available, or by clearing the App's data.

## 4. Network Access

Core puzzle gameplay works offline. The App uses an internet connection only to load ads and related advertising services.

---

## 5. Permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or files. It uses the following standard permissions, mainly required by the advertising SDK:

- **Internet / network state** — to load ads
- **Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`)** — used by the advertising SDK as described in Section 3

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

---

## 7. Data Security

Gameplay progress stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Data handled by Google AdMob is transmitted using encryption in transit, as described in Google's policies.

---

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

- **Gameplay progress:** delete it at any time via **Settings → Apps → Block Escape X → Storage → Clear data**, or by uninstalling the App. We have no copy of it.
- **Advertising data:** retained by Google according to its own policies. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings as described in Section 3.
